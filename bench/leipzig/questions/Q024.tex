Consider the following double cover of graphs: $G_1$ has 5 vertices and edges $\{(1,2),(1,3),(2,3),(2,3),(2,4),(3,5),(4,5),(4,5)\}$, and $G_2$ has $3$ vertices and edges $\{(1,2),(2,2),(2,3),(3,3)\}$. The map on vertices is given by $1 \mapsto 1, 2\mapsto 2, 3 \mapsto 2, 4\mapsto 3, 5\mapsto 3$. Compute the signed graphic matroid of this double cover, and give the set of bases.
