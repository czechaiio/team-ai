The circle graph with 8 edges has the property that on the Bergman fan of its graphic matroid the coarse fan structure coincides with the minimal nested set structure.

Count up to isomorphism the number of all simple connected graphs $G$ with 8 edges such that the associated graphic matroid $M$ has the property that it is connected and that on the Bergman fan of $M$ the coarse fan structure coincides with the minimal nested set fan structure.
