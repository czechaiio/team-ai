Let $[L]$ be a general point of the Grassmannian $Gr(6, 17)$, and let $X$ denote the visible contour compactification of the corresponding hyperplane arrangement. Then $X$ is naturally embedded in the torus-orbit closure of $[L]$. Let $E$ denote the conormal bundle of $X$ in this toric variety. Compute the degree of $c_{\dim X}(E)$.
