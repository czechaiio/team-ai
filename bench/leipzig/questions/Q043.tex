Let $X = \operatorname{Seg}(\mathbb{P}^3 \times \mathbb{P}^3)
\subset \mathbb{P}(\mathbb{C}^4 \otimes \mathbb{C}^4)$ be the Segre variety of rank-1 matrices, $\sigma_2(X)$ its 2nd secant variety (consisting of rank-2 matrices), and
\[
  u = e_1^{\otimes 2} + e_2^{\otimes 2} + \tfrac{1}{100}\,e_3^{\otimes 2}
      + \tfrac{1}{100}\, e_4^{\otimes 2}
  \;\in\; \mathbb{R}^{4 \times 4}.
\]
Consider the polynomial optimization problem
\begin{align}
  \min_{\substack{a^{(k)},\, b^{(k)}\,\in\,\mathbb{R}^4\\k=1,2}}
  &f(a,b)
  \;:=\;
  \sum_{i,j=1}^{4}
  \Bigl(
    u_{ij}
    - \sum_{k=1}^{2} a^{(k)}_i\, b^{(k)}_j
  \Bigr)^{\!2}\\
  \text{subject to } &f(a,b) \le 1/50.
\end{align}
Use the standard Moment-SOS hierarchy for this Polynomial Optimization Problem (POP), where the d-th level optimizes over polynomials of degree at most 2d. If the hierarchy has finite convergence, set s=1. Otherwise, set s=-1. Compute $(s\cdot f_2^*, s\cdot f_3^*, s\cdot f_4^*)$, where $f_{d}^*$ denotes the optimal value of the d-th level of the hierarchy.
