Let $K_1$ and $K_2$ be any alternating knots with Seifert genus 1 and signature 0. Suppose that $\det(K_1)=2001$ and $\det(K_2)=2025$. Let $M_i$ be the exterior of $K_i$ for $i=1,2$. Let $M$ be the manifold obtained by gluing $M_1$ to $M_2$ along their boundaries, identifying the meridian of one knot with the Seifert longitude of the other knot and vice versa. What is the dimension of the hat version of Heegaard Floer homology of $M$ with coefficients in the field of two elements?
