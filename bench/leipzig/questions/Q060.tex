Call a finite dimensional algebra $A$ self-orthogonal if every indecomposable $A$-module $M$ is self-orthogonal meaning $Ext_A^i(M,M)=0$ for all $i>0$. Consider the class of all connected cyclic Nakayama algebras given by quiver and admissible relations over a field $K$ with 8 simple modules that have dominant dimension at least two and are self-orthogonal. What is the sum of the vector space dimensions of those algebras?
