Let $K$ be the knot $9_{46}$, $n$ an integer and $M_n$ the Dehn filling of $K$ along the slope $1/n$. The two strong inversions of $K$ extend to $M_n$. Let $K^1_n$ and $K^2_n$ be the branch sets in the respective quotients. For $i=1,2$, let $g_i$ be the limit of $Kh(K^i_n)-Kh(K^i_{n-1})$ as $n$ goes to $\infty$, where $Kh(L)$ denotes the (unreduced) Khovanov homology of a link $L$.  What is the dimension of the (hat version of) Heegaard Floer homology of $M_{g_1\cdot g_2}$? All homology theories are taken with coefficients in the field of 2 elements.
