Let $K$ be an algebraically closed and complete extension field of $\mathbb{Q}_p$ and let $X$ be a smooth, proper, connected curve over $K$ of genus $g$. We look at the associated diamond $X^\diamond$ defined by Peter Scholze. Let $\mathcal{O}$ be its structure sheaf. $X^\diamond$ carries an etale topology which we denote by $et$ and a quasi-pro-etale topology which we denote by $qproet$.

Look at the inclusion $Ext^1_{et}(\mathcal{O}, \mathcal{O}) \rightarrow Ext^1_{qproet}(\mathcal{O}, \mathcal{O})$ given by self-extensions of the structure sheaf on $X^\diamond$ with respect to these two topologies. Compute the dimension of the cokernel of this inclusion as a $K$-vector space. If it is not finite dimensional, give back $\infty$.
