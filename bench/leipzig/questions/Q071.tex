Let $x$ be a point in the complex Grassmannian $G(k,n)$, and let $M$ be the corresponding matroid. The Chow class of $M$ is defined as the class of the torus orbit closure $\overline{(\mathbb C^*)^n x}$ in the Chow ring of $G(k,n)$.

Expand the Chow class of the non-nesting rook matroid (arXiv: 2410.00127) defined by $UUUURURRRRUR$ and $RUURUURRRRUU$ as a linear combination of Schubert cycles $s_{\lambda}$.
