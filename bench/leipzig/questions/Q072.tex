Let $f_1$, ..., $f_{12}$ be 12 linear forms on a 6-dimensional complex vector space V which define a hyperplane arrangement that realizes the matroid FP12. Let W be the subspace of rational functions on PV spanned by elements of the forms $\prod_{i \in B} f_i^{-1}$, where $B$ is a basis of FP12. Choose a generic hyperplane in $V$, and let $X$ be the kernel of the restriction map from $W$ to this generic hyperplane. Compute the dimension of $X$.
