Suppose I have two functions \( f_1: \mathbb R^d \to \mathbb R^n \), and \( f_2: \mathbb R^n \to \mathbb R \). The functions are of the form \( f_i(x) = \max(A_i x, B_i x) \). Here, \( A_1, B_1 \) are \( n \times d \) matrices with real entries, and \( A_2, B_2 \) are \( 1 \times n \) matrices with non-negative real entries. The composition $f(x) = f_2(f_1(x))$ is convex, continuous, piecewise-linear and positively homogeneous. Its dual polytope is denoted $P_f$. For $d = 2$, $n = 1003$, determine $\max\{ \#\, \text{vertices } P_f : f \text{ is as described above} \}$.
