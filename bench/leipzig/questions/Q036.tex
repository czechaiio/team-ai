Let $X$ be the Segre embedding of $(\mathbb{P}^1)^{\times 3}$ in $\mathbb{P}^{7}$, and consider its affine cone $CX$ in $\mathbb{C}^8$. Fix coordinates $(x_1,\ldots,x_8)$ for $\mathbb{C}^8$. What is the number of complex critical points of the function $f(x)=2(x_1-u_1)^2+\sum_{i=2}^8(x_i-u_i)^2$ from a generic point $u=(u_1,\ldots,u_8)\in\mathbb{C}^8$ restricted to $CX$?
