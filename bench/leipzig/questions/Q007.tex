Let $R$ be the ring of polynomials in $20$ variables and let $I$ be the ideal of $R$ consisting of functions vanishing on the set of points in $\mathbf{C}^{20}$ where some $6$ coordinates are equal to one another. Finally, let $\mathbf{C}$ be the $R$-module which is $\mathbf{C}$ as a vector space, upon which the variables all act by $0$. What is the dimension of $\mathrm{Tor}^2_R(I,\mathbf{C})$?
