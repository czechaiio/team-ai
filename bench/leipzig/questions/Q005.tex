Let $A$ be the connected cyclic Nakayama algebra with Kupisch series $[2,3]$ and let $B$ be the Auslander algebra of $A$. Let $C$ be the algebra $B/I$ where $I$ is the two-sided ideal given by the socle of the direct sum of all indecomposable projective-injective $B$-modules (as a submodule of the regular module). What is the sum of all delooping levels of the indecomposable $C$-modules?
