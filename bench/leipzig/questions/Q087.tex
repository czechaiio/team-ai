Let $KQ$ be the path algebra of the Dynkin quiver of type $A_5$ (so $Q$ has 5 vertices) with linear orientation. Let $A$ be the Koszul dual algebra of the Auslander algebra of $KQ$. How many indecomposable spherical $A$-modules are there? Here a module $M$ is called spherical (in the sense of Auslander-Bridger) if $M$ has finite projective dimension equal to $n$ and $Ext_A^i(M,A)=0$ for $i=1,...,n-1$ with the convention that every module of projective dimension less than or equal to 1 is spherical.
