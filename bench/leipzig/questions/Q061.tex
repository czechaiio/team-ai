Consider the following symmetric tensor $T\in \mathbb C^{6\times 6\times 6}$:
\begin{align*}
T_{:,:,1} &= \begin{pmatrix} 25 & 20 & 17 & 15 & -16 & -6 \\ 20 & -4 & 2 & -13 & 30 & 27 \\ 17 & 2 & 59 & -28 & 54 & 60 \\ 15 & -13 & -28 & 19 & -2 & 0 \\ -16 & 30 & 54 & -2 & 8 & 15 \\ -6 & 27 & 60 & 0 & 15 & 54 \end{pmatrix}, & T_{:,:,2} &= \begin{pmatrix} 20 & -4 & 2 & -13 & 30 & 27 \\ -4 & 28 & 23 & 35 & -36 & 0 \\ 2 & 23 & 23 & 26 & -18 & -9 \\ -13 & 35 & 26 & 55 & -51 & 3 \\ 30 & -36 & -18 & -51 & 54 & 3 \\ 27 & 0 & -9 & 3 & 3 & -12 \end{pmatrix},\\[1ex]
T_{:,:,3} &= \begin{pmatrix} 17 & 2 & 59 & -28 & 54 & 60 \\ 2 & 23 & 23 & 26 & -18 & -9 \\ 59 & 23 & 27 & 32 & -13 & 18 \\ -28 & 26 & 32 & 62 & -42 & -15 \\ 54 & -18 & -13 & -42 & 49 & 21 \\ 60 & -9 & 18 & -15 & 21 & 12 \end{pmatrix}, & T_{:,:,4} &= \begin{pmatrix} 15 & -13 & -28 & 19 & -2 & 0 \\ -13 & 35 & 26 & 55 & -51 & 3 \\ -28 & 26 & 32 & 62 & -42 & -15 \\ 19 & 55 & 62 & 72 & -43 & 39 \\ -2 & -51 & -42 & -43 & 37 & -27 \\ 0 & 3 & -15 & 39 & -27 & -21 \end{pmatrix},\\[1ex]
T_{:,:,5} &= \begin{pmatrix} -16 & 30 & 54 & -2 & 8 & 15 \\ 30 & -36 & -18 & -51 & 54 & 3 \\ 54 & -18 & -13 & -42 & 49 & 21 \\ -2 & -51 & -42 & -43 & 37 & -27 \\ 8 & 54 & 49 & 37 & -29 & 33 \\ 15 & 3 & 21 & -27 & 33 & 15 \end{pmatrix}, & T_{:,:,6} &= \begin{pmatrix} -6 & 27 & 60 & 0 & 15 & 54 \\ 27 & 0 & -9 & 3 & 3 & -12 \\ 60 & -9 & 18 & -15 & 21 & 12 \\ 0 & 3 & -15 & 39 & -27 & -21 \\ 15 & 3 & 21 & -27 & 33 & 15 \\ 54 & -12 & 12 & -21 & 15 & 9 \end{pmatrix}.
\end{align*}
Determine the rank $r$ and the unique minimum rank decomposition of $T$, so that $T = \sum_{i=1}^{r} a_i^{\otimes 3}$, where $a_1,\ldots,a_r \in \mathbb C^{6}$. As your answer, submit the tuple $(r, \sum_{i=1}^r |a_{i,3}|^2)$, consisting of the rank $r$ and the ``checksum'' of $3$rd coordinates $a_{i,3}$ of the vectors in the minimum rank decomposition.
