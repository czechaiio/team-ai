For a Dyck path $d$ of semilength $n$, consider its bounce path. The ``reversing bounce path'' of $d$ is the path that arises by reversing $d$, considering its bounce path, and reversing it again. For example, for $d=(()())$, its bounce path is $(())()$ and its reverse bounce path is $()(())$. How many Dyck paths are there for $n=10$, whose bounce path is equal to their reversing bounce path?
