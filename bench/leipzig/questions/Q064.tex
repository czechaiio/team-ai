For a connected Nakayama algebra $A$ with $n$ simple modules with associated Kupisch series $c(A)=[c_0,\dots,c_{n-1}]$, define $\Sigma A$ as the sum of all entries of the Kupisch series. Let $L_n$ be the set of all connected Nakayama algebras $A$ (linear and cyclic) with $n$ simple modules that are higher Auslander algebras (meaning the global dimension and the dominant dimension of $A$ are finite and coincide) and for which the unique cycle in the resolution quiver of $A$ consists of exactly 1 vertex. Compute $\sum_{A \in L_n}\Sigma A$ for $n=20$.
