Consider the moduli space of 7 labeled points in linearly general position in the projective plane. This is the open Grassmannian $\mathrm{Gr}(3,7)$ where all 35 Pl\"ucker coordinates are non-zero modulo the action of the 7-dimensional torus. We are interested in the log canonical embedding of this moduli space. What is the dimension of the projective space where this log canonical model lives?
