Let $X$ be the variety of symmetric $6\times 6$ matrices of rank at most $4$, viewed as a subvariety in a projective space $\mathbb{P}^{20}$ of dimension $20$. We are interested in the coisotropic hypersurface $\mathrm{Ch}_3(X)$ which consists of all linear spaces of dimension $5$ in $\mathbb{P}^{20}$ which fail to intersect $X$ transversally. What is the degree of this hypersurface in Pl\"ucker coordinates on the Grassmannian $\mathrm{Gr}(5,\mathbb{P}^{20})$?
