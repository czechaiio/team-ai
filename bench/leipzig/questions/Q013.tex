Fix two positive integers $r$ and $m$. Consider the family of subsets $C_i=\{2i+1,...,2i+r\}$ where $i$ ranges from $0$ to $m-1$. Let $M$ be the matroid of rank $r$ on $n=2(m-1)+r$ elements whose non-bases are exactly the $m$ subsets $C_0,...,C_{m-1}$. Using the notation of the paper \url{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.04980} express the unnormalised symmetrized class $[X_{M,sym}]$ in the basis of the symmetric part of the degree $n-r$-part of the Chow ring of the permutohedron given by square free monomials in the differences $H_k:=L_{n-1-k} - L_{n-k}$ (where $k$ runs from $1$ to $n-1$ and we use convention $L_0=0$). As a formula of $r$ and $m$ please give me the coefficient of the monomial $H_{r-1}\cdot \prod_{k=1}^{n-r-1}H_{n-k}$ in this expression.
