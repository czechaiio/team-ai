Let $Z$ be the free graded commutative algebra with one generator in each degree, except in degrees $1$, $4$, and $6$. For a natural number $k$, let $X_k$ be the projective toric variety parametrized by the degree $k$ monomials in $Z$ (inside $\mathbb P(Z_k)$). What is the largest $k$ such that $X_k$ is normal?
