Let $[L]$ be the point of the Grassmannian $Gr(14, 105)$ corresponding to the $14$-dimensional braid arrangement, and let $X$ be the torus-orbit closure of $[L]$. Compute the coefficient of the Schubert variety corresponding to the partition $(91)^{12}, 78, 0$ in the expansion of the homology class of $X$ in the Schubert basis.
